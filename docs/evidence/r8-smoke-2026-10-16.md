## C5 — scheduled FX closure suppression and post-filter offline replay

**Code commit:** `2f5c3ca` on `engine/handover-2026-10`. The weekly closure is the UTC half-open interval **Friday 21:00 through Sunday 21:00**; the fixed holiday closures cover all of **December 25** and **January 1** in UTC. Sunday 21:00 is the reopening instant. A bar whose open timestamp is inside a closure is excluded from R8 analysis and its R8 lookback windows. Signal eligibility also checks the candle's decision-close instant, so a last pre-close bar cannot emit a signal exactly as the market closes. This follows the defined boundary without changing the strategy gates or predicates.

At the engine pipeline, a closed-time pseudo-bar returns no strategy decision, `metadata.status_reason = MARKET_CLOSED`, and `final_action = NO_ACTION`. The live `ScanService` returns `NO_TRADE` / `MARKET_CLOSED` before calling a provider; automatic scans short-circuit as well. It also avoids fetching the last candle that overlaps a closure at the exact Sunday reopening. Provider data is not requested while the market is closed. Closed-time outcome resolution is held until the market reopens.
