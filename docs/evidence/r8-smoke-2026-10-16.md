## F1 correction and regression test

The date-only Twelve Data `end_date` stopped at 00:00 UTC on that date. `fetchHistoricalCandles` now requests `end_date=YYYY-MM-DD 23:59:59` in UTC, then clips returned candle timestamps to the half-open interval `[start date 00:00, day after end date 00:00)`. This also removes any provider-returned candle outside the requested range. No strategy, signal, gate, threshold, or weekend filter changed.

The mock regression is in `test/r8_backtest_core.test.ts`. It models the 30-day 5m range split into the two real request chunks (Sep 1–15 and Sep 16–30), checks each request's `23:59:59` end, injects the next-day boundary candle to verify clipping, and asserts all 8,640 expected 5m timestamps are present with no interval gap through Sep 30 23:55 UTC.
